# Privacy Policy — Canada Business Compliance (Frappe/ERPNext app)

**Effective date:** October 18, 2026
**Publisher:** IPCONNEX Services Inc., Montréal, Québec ("IPCONNEX", "we")

This policy explains how IPCONNEX handles personal information in connection with the Canada Business Compliance app (the "App"), its Frappe Cloud Marketplace listing and our support. It follows Québec's *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector* (Law 25) and the federal *PIPEDA*, where it applies.

## 1. The App sends no data to IPCONNEX

The App runs entirely inside your own Frappe/ERPNext site. In the current version (v0.3.4), it contains **no telemetry, analytics, licence checks, update checks, error reporting or calls to outside services**, including government services. It does not send any data to IPCONNEX or to anyone else. If a future version adds an outbound connection, we will describe it here before it is released.

## 2. Who controls what

- **Your site data.** Customer, supplier, item, invoice and tax data in your site, including any exemption or registration numbers you enter, stays in your site. **You control that data** and are responsible for it under privacy law.
- **Hosting.** If your site is hosted on Frappe Cloud, Frappe Technologies hosts that data under its own terms and privacy policy. IPCONNEX has no access to it through the App.
- **Support access.** IPCONNEX is not your service provider for your site data, unless you choose to give us access for support. In that case we act only on your written request (email to admin@ipconnex.com or a GitHub issue), access only what is needed for the issue, use a temporary or named account where possible, log the access, do not copy data out of your site unless you agree, and tell you when we are done so you can remove the access. We will promptly tell you about any confidentiality incident affecting your data.

## 3. Personal information IPCONNEX handles

We handle only a limited amount of personal information:

| Source | Information | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| GitHub Issues | Your GitHub username and profile, and whatever you write in the issue | Answer questions, fix bugs, improve the App |
| Support email | Name, email address, organization, message content | Answer your request |
| Frappe Cloud Marketplace (if shared by Frappe) | Name, email, organization and site name | Manage your installation and contact you about the App |

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for profiling or targeted advertising.

**GitHub Issues are public.** Anything posted there is visible to everyone and is governed by GitHub's privacy statement. Do not post personal information, customer data, credentials or confidential information there.

## 4. Consent

We collect this information when you contact us, open an issue or install the App through the marketplace, and we use it only for the purposes above. You can withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits (for example, records we must keep for tax purposes).

## 5. Retention

- Support emails: up to 3 years after the request is closed.
- GitHub Issues: they stay on GitHub until you or we delete them. We can delete or edit issues in our repository on request.

We then destroy or anonymize the information.

## 6. Communication outside Québec

Some of our providers are located, or store information, outside Québec or Canada, so your information may be communicated outside Québec:
- **GitHub** (GitHub, Inc., United States) for issues;
- **Frappe Technologies** (India; hosting regions vary) for marketplace and hosting;
- our **email provider**, which may store information outside Québec or Canada.

Before such communication, we assess whether the information will receive adequate protection, as required by law, and we rely on contractual terms with these providers. Information held outside Canada may be subject to the laws of those countries.

## 7. Security

We protect personal information with reasonable measures, including access limited to staff who need it, multi-factor authentication and secure password management. No method is completely secure.

## 8. Your rights

You may ask to access your personal information, to have it corrected, to withdraw consent, or to receive it in a structured, commonly used technological format where applicable. We will reply within 30 days. To make a request, contact the person in charge below.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may file a complaint with the **Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec** (cai.gouv.qc.ca) or, where PIPEDA applies, the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada** (priv.gc.ca).

## 9. Cookies

The App sets no cookies of its own. Your Frappe site uses Frappe's standard session cookies, which you or your host control. GitHub, Frappe Cloud and ipconnex.com have their own cookie policies.
